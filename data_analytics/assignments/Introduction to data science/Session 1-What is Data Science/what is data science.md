
**1.List 3 apps you use daily (such as Instagram, Zomato, Paytm) and describe one way each app uses data to improve your experience.**

**SOLUTION**

1. Instagram: Instagram uses data from your likes, follows, watch time, and engagement patterns to personalize your feed and suggest posts, reels, and accounts that match your interests. This makes your experience more engaging and relevant.

2. Zomato: Zomato analyzes your location, previous orders, cuisines you like, and restaurant ratings to recommend nearby restaurants and deals that are most likely to suit your preferences. This helps you discover food options faster and more conveniently.

3. Paytm: Paytm uses data from your transactions, spending habits, and payment history to show personalized offers, cashback deals, and reminders based on your usage pattern. This improves convenience and encourages relevant services at the right time.

**3.Imagine you work for a music streaming app like Spotify. Write a short scenario (3-4 sentences) describing how data-driven decision making could help the app recommend new songs to users.**

**SOLUTION**

If a user listens to a lot of indie pop songs with upbeat tempo and acoustic instruments, the app can analyze their listening history, favorite artists, and similar users’ preferences to recommend songs with a matching style. Data from skip rates, repeat plays, and playlist saves helps the system understand which songs users actually enjoy. By combining this information with trends in genre popularity, the app can suggest fresh tracks that fit the user’s taste while still introducing variety. This data-driven approach improves user engagement and keeps the listening experience personalized and enjoyable.
