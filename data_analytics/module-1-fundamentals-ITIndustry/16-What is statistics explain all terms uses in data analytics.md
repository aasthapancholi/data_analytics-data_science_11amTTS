# What is statistics?

Statistics is the branch of mathematics that deals with collecting, organizing, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data.

It helps us understand patterns, trends, relationships, and anomalies in data.

In data analytics, statistics is used to make sense of data and support decision-making.

# Why statistics is important in data analytics

Statistics is important because it helps analysts:
- summarize large datasets
- identify patterns and trends
- test assumptions
- compare groups
- measure uncertainty
- draw meaningful conclusions

# Basic statistical terms used in data analytics

## 1. Data

Data is a collection of facts, numbers, observations, or information used for analysis.

Examples:
- sales numbers
- test scores
- customer feedback
- website traffic

## 2. Population

A population is the entire group of people or items that we want to study.

Example:
- all students in a college
- all customers of a shop

## 3. Sample

A sample is a smaller part of the population selected for analysis.

Example:
- 100 students selected from 1000 students

## 4. Variable

A variable is a characteristic or value that can change.

Examples:
- age
- salary
- product price
- sales amount

## 5. Qualitative data

Qualitative data describes qualities or categories.

Examples:
- gender
- color
- product category
- customer feedback

## 6. Quantitative data

Quantitative data is numerical and measurable.

Examples:
- height
- income
- temperature
- sales revenue

## 7. Descriptive statistics

Descriptive statistics summarize the main features of a dataset.

Examples:
- mean
- median
- mode
- standard deviation

## 8. Inferential statistics

Inferential statistics uses sample data to make conclusions about a larger population.

Examples:
- hypothesis testing
- confidence intervals
- regression analysis

## 9. Mean

Mean is the average value of a dataset.

Formula:

Mean = sum of all values / total number of values

## 10. Median

Median is the middle value when numbers are arranged in ascending or descending order.

## 11. Mode

Mode is the value that appears most often in a dataset.

## 12. Range

Range is the difference between the highest and lowest values.

## 13. Variance

Variance measures how far data points are spread from the mean.

## 14. Standard deviation

Standard deviation measures the amount of variation or dispersion in a dataset.

A low standard deviation means data is close to the mean.
A high standard deviation means data is widely spread out.

## 15. Correlation

Correlation shows the relationship between two variables.

Example:
- relationship between study hours and marks

It can be:
- positive
- negative
- zero correlation

## 16. Regression

Regression is used to predict the value of one variable based on another variable.

Example:
- predicting sales based on advertising spend

## 17. Probability

Probability is the chance or likelihood of an event happening.

Example:
- probability of rain = 70%

## 18. Hypothesis testing

Hypothesis testing is used to check whether a claim about a population is true based on sample data.

Examples:
- does a new marketing strategy increase sales?
- is the average sales greater than last month?

## 19. P-value

The p-value helps decide whether a result is statistically significant.

If p-value is small, the result is likely not due to chance.

## 20. Confidence interval

A confidence interval gives a range of values that likely contains the true population value.

## 21. Distribution

Distribution shows how values are spread across a dataset.

Examples:
- normal distribution
- skewed distribution
- binomial distribution

## 22. Outliers

Outliers are values that are unusually high or low compared to the rest of the data.

They can affect analysis and should be investigated.

# Common statistical concepts in data analytics

## Descriptive analytics

Descriptive analytics explains what happened in the past.

Examples:
- monthly sales report
- website traffic summary
- average customer age

## Diagnostic analytics

Diagnostic analytics explains why something happened.

Example:
- why did sales drop in July?

## Predictive analytics

Predictive analytics predicts what may happen in the future.

Example:
- predicting next month sales

## Prescriptive analytics

Prescriptive analytics suggests actions to take.

Example:
- recommend discount strategy to increase sales

# Why statistics is used in data analytics

Statistics helps in:
- summarizing business performance
- understanding customer behavior
- improving products and services
- reducing uncertainty in decisions
- measuring success or failure of strategies

# Example

Suppose a company collects monthly sales data:

20, 25, 30, 35, 40

- mean = 30
- median = 30
- range = 20
- standard deviation = around 7.9

This helps the business understand the average sales and how spread out the values are.

# Summary

Statistics is the science of collecting and interpreting data.

It plays a major role in data analytics by helping us summarize, compare, predict, and make decisions based on data.

Important statistical terms include mean, median, mode, range, variance, standard deviation, correlation, regression, probability, hypothesis testing, and confidence interval.

# Final thought

Statistics is the foundation of data analytics. Without statistics, data would just be raw numbers. With statistics, raw data becomes meaningful information that supports informed business and technical decisions.
