# What is LAN, WAN, and MAN?

These are types of computer networks based on their geographical coverage and purpose.

# LAN (Local Area Network)

LAN stands for Local Area Network.

It is a network that connects computers and devices in a small area such as:
- home
- office
- school
- building
- lab

Features of LAN:
- small area coverage
- high speed
- low cost
- shared resources like printers and files

Examples:
- Wi-Fi at home
- office computer network
- school lab network

Advantages:
- fast communication
- easy file sharing
- low setup cost
- secure environment

Disadvantages:
- limited coverage area
- not suitable for long-distance communication

# WAN (Wide Area Network)

WAN stands for Wide Area Network.

It connects devices or networks over a large geographical area such as:
- cities
- countries
- continents

WANs are used to connect multiple LANs together.

Features of WAN:
- large area coverage
- slower than LAN
- uses leased lines, satellites, or internet connections
- supports long-distance communication

Examples:
- internet
- bank networks
- multinational company networks

Advantages:
- connects distant locations
- supports global communication
- allows sharing of data across countries

Disadvantages:
- expensive
- more complex setup
- slower than LAN

# MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

MAN stands for Metropolitan Area Network.

It covers a larger area than a LAN but smaller than a WAN.

It usually connects networks across a city or metropolitan area.

Features of MAN:
- covers city or metropolitan region
- larger than LAN
- smaller than WAN
- often used by government institutions, banks, and large organizations

Examples:
- city network of offices
- network connecting multiple campuses in a city
- public service network in a metro area

Advantages:
- covers a city-sized area
- good for inter-city connectivity
- high-speed compared to WAN

Disadvantages:
- more expensive than LAN
- requires more maintenance
- more complex than LAN

# Difference between LAN, WAN, and MAN

| Network Type | Coverage Area | Example |
|-------------|---------------|---------|
| LAN | Small area like home or office | Wi-Fi network in a school |
| MAN | City or metropolitan area | City government network |
| WAN | Large area like country or globe | Internet |

# Summary

- LAN is a local network used in a small area.
- MAN is a network used in a city or metropolitan area.
- WAN is a network used across large distances and countries.

These networks help devices communicate and share information efficiently.

# Final thought

LAN, MAN, and WAN are important types of networks used in modern communication. They connect people, devices, and organizations in different geographic areas and make global communication possible.
